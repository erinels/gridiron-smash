Football. Wrestling. Soccer. Basketball. Four games in one.

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE: Arrow keys or W A S D. Press two at once to run diagonally.

SPACE: Spin away from tacklers (football), PUNCH (wrestling), SHOOT (soccer and basketball)

E: Throw the ball (football), BIG SLAM (wrestling), BIG KICK (soccer), BIG SHOT (basketball)

SHIFT: Run super fast

ON A PHONE: Hold the left side to steer. Big buttons on the right do the moves. Tap RUN to go fast.

In soccer, run at the ball and it sticks to your feet. A yellow ring shows you have it.

In basketball, run into the gold ring under the hoop and press SHOOT to DUNK it. Shoot from far back for 3 points.

In wrestling you can do a BELT RUN: beat 5 wrestlers, one after the other. The last one is the BOSS. Or pick just one rival to fight.

Player 2 uses I J K L to move, G to hit, H for special.
